About Our Firm

Mammodi Leshilo Attorneys is an African law firm founded in the Republic of South Africa and 100% black owned and controlled. We provide comprehensive, integrated and in-depth legal services throughout the country.

Our director has over 20 years of experience working on some of the most transformative and significant matters in the country. The firm was founded on 17 November 2023 in Polokwane with strategic location in the heartland of Limpopo Province.

We are committed to exceptional client service consistently delivered through every client experience. We have extensive experience in advising on matters spanning the country's provinces, regions and languages.

Our aim is to deliver in the manner that best suits our clients' requirements, preferences and pricing structures. We bring together expertise and resources, offering proven experience in working with governments and their agencies.

Company Profile

Director Profile

Mammodi Cedrick Leshilo is an admitted attorney of the High Court of South Africa since 2005. He holds a B.Proc qualification and has extensive experience in labour, commercial, and general law.

He has acted for and against the Road Accident Fund, various municipalities, government departments, and has represented clients at CCMA, bargaining councils, Labour Court, and Labour Appeal Court.

Mission Statement

The mission of our firm is to promote affirmative action as well as black empowerment for purposes of contributing to a stable community in the Republic of South Africa.

We are committed to building long-term relationships founded in a values-driven culture, and providing to-the-point, jargon-free advice to clients.
